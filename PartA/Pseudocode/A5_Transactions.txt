A5 TRANSFER
ALGORITHM TRANSFER(source,destination,amount)
    REQUIRE amount > 0; save autoCommit; set autoCommit FALSE
    TRY debit source and require one row changed; credit destination and require one row changed; COMMIT
    CATCH failure: ROLLBACK; rethrow
    FINALLY restore old autoCommit
END
Two auto-commit updates can leave money debited without credit. Use constraints and consistent lock order.
